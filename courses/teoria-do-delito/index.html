    Editorial record and source boundaries

    This web edition preserves the selected editable guide rather than shortening it into an outline. Its structure has been checked against the course plan, with sampled content review. That does not establish that every legal proposition or every classroom reading is covered or verified.

    The original Desktop PDF and the editable files have separate version histories. This edition records the specific inputs and transformations used. Brandão and Roxin material is presented as supplementary synthesis, not as additional scheduled classes.
