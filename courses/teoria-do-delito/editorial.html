Study Lab / first web edition

What this edition is built from.

Editorial review: 8 September 2026

This is an AI-assisted study guide assembled from course materials and supplementary reading. It is not a transcript of lectures, an official university publication, or a claim that every proposition has been independently verified.

Why this course came first

We compared three existing guides: Teoria do Delito, Metodologia Jurídica and Controle de Constitucionalidade. Teoria offered the clearest semester structure and the fewest identified publication obstacles. Methodology has unresolved links to supplemental dossiers and gaps in assigned-source coverage. Constitutional Review needs language repairs and renewed checks of several case-law developments.

The chosen guide is substantial: 15 study units, five doctrinal supplements, a course map and a master study guide. The web edition retains those materials instead of replacing them with a shorter overview.

Versions and sources

The Desktop reference edition is a 456-page PDF, Teoria do Delito — Complete Study Pack (Louro Classico). The editable Markdown has a different metadata history. Comparisons found effectively identical normalized bodies between the editable files and the preserved release candidate for units 1–19, with a small difference in unit 20. PDF text extraction also matched closely, allowing for layout and appended material.

The web edition therefore uses the editable Markdown bodies as its input and records their hashes. The course-map links to private vault records are replaced by this web edition’s source and coverage records; the underlying private records are not reproduced in full. The PDF remains a reference edition; its “Complete” filename is not adopted as a verified completeness claim.

  • Course plan: PLANO TEORIA DO DELITO 2026 (2), DIR01199, 2026/2.
  • Cláudio Brandão: Teorias da Conduta, the supplied seven-page course extract.
  • Claus Roxin: Política Criminal e Sistema Jurídico-penal, supplied 2002 edition, 60 digital pages. OCR was used as a search aid; the scanned pages remain the authority for exact quotation.
  • Brazilian statutory anchors: official consolidated Código Penal.

Source books, course PDFs and private working files are not redistributed here. The guide's own source qualifications remain part of the reading.

What was checked—and what remains open

The audit mapped every plan block to the guide, inspected chapter structure and sampled substantive content. Five high-impact areas were checked: Brandão's concept of conduct; Roxin's relationship between criminal policy and legal certainty; his action/duty distinction; his treatment of responsibility and withdrawal; and Brazilian statutory anchors for causation, intent, attempt, mistake, justification, imputability and participation.

Those samples supported the guide's presentation, including its distinction between comparative German theory and Brazilian law. They do not establish complete accuracy across the whole guide. Exact doctrinal pinpoints, every explanatory example and subsequent changes in law remain matters for review.

Web transformations

Machine metadata is kept out of the reading. Markdown headings, tables and annotations become semantic HTML; internal navigation receives stable web links. The three original flowcharts are rendered as diagrams with textual relationship alternatives. Reading order and substantive prose are preserved, with specific conversion changes recorded in the build manifest.
