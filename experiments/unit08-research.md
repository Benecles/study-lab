# Unit 08 — reference research and full-lesson rebuild

Date: 2026-09-10. Status: candidate for review, not an approved house style.

## What failed

The previous isolated specimen was not the complete lesson requested. Adding blue bookcloth outside beige paper added another visible container, rather than making the page itself convincing. The user rejected this, along with artificial design commentary. Preserve that rejection: do not cite the previous pass as an accepted direction.

## References inspected

### Miranda — Paper Portfolio
https://www.niccolomiranda.com/

Inspected the live desktop opening. A continuous paper surface, dense editorial rules, large type and image placement make the screen read as printed matter. The identity does not depend on every paragraph sitting inside a separate card. Useful here: continuous stock and confident hierarchy. Do not copy the extreme headline compression or force the reader to rotate a phone. Our lesson contains far more sustained reading.

### Marijana Pavlinić — Digital Stamp Collection
https://marijanapav.com/stamps

Inspected the live album opening. Page perforations, faint ruling, collection tabs, printed stamps and annotations use different marks on a shared surface. Its creators' account describes how inspection, metadata and feedback take forms belonging to the album:
https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/09/building-an-interactive-digital-stamp-collection-with-shaders-postcards-and-playful-inspection/

Transfer the method, not the stamp assets: a case analysis can feel attached to its facts; a classification can be a pencilled intervention on a diagram. Controls must still perform the action their physical form suggests. A loupe is appropriate for inspecting stamp artwork; it would add little to normal legal paragraphs.

### Nicky Case — The Evolution of Trust
https://ncase.me/trust/

Inspected the live opening screen. A limited vocabulary of drawn people, connections, handwritten lettering and a plainly drawn play control establishes a coherent world on white. This is useful evidence against the assumption that analog identity requires beige or a dark backing. The interactive explanatory approach is a separate reference from its particular drawing style; the opening alone does not prove how every later interaction works.

### The Pudding — process
https://pudding.cool/process/how-to-make-dope-shit-part-3/
https://pudding.cool/about/

Read the team's account of visual essays and experimentation. The useful process decision is to design the explanation and writing together, and to allow unsuccessful visual approaches to be discarded. Here, temporal phases need a sequence; statutory requirements need conjunction; competing criteria need a common question; evidence needs an explicit counter-reading. A visual treatment should make the relationship easier to see.

### Sara Soueidan — SVG paper texture
https://tympanus.net/codrops/2019/02/19/svg-filter-effects-creating-texture-with-feturbulence/

Read the author's technical explanation of noise plus diffuse lighting. Tested a small repeating SVG behind the text. First trial used strongly unequal frequencies and looked streaked, closer to wood than uncoated stock. Rejected it. Raised both frequencies, reduced relief and opacity. Keep the result behind glyphs; never apply distortion or noise to the reading layer itself.

### Tomasz Szmajda — illustrated spatial portfolio
https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/11/sketching-the-impossible-a-3d-portfolio-built-without-a-single-3d-model/

Read the creator's case study, not a full live walkthrough. Flat illustrated surfaces can support a coherent spatial world; painted hover feedback can communicate clickability. This remains a future exploration rather than a reason to add a 3D engine to this lesson. A long study text should retain normal scrolling, selection and links.

## Composition trials in this rebuild

1. Flat editorial draft: continuous near-white paper, large heading, numbered margin and full-width inserts. Removed the nested frames, but the substrate was too visually absent.
2. Lit directional fiber: surface identity became visible, but long streaks competed with the reading. Rejected after browser inspection.
3. Fine uncoated stock with local objects: quieter continuous background; cream case sheets with visible layered edges; green ruled causal drawing; red/green impossibility comparison; yellow marginal qualifications. Current candidate.

The execution criteria moved from repeated definition rows to a four-part composition around one question. Evidence now gives the proposed inference and its caution separate reading positions. Ordinary prose remains where it carries qualifications that a diagram would obscure. Case facts remain visible and their analysis can be opened independently. This is a complete nine-section lesson built from all fourteen source sections, not a new isolated demo.

## Rules to carry forward

- Decide what the physical substrate is before adding decorations. It can extend to the viewport edges.
- Give depth to a distinct attached object; do not shadow every paragraph.
- Keep paper texture, printing and later annotation visibly different. Avoid an identical filter over everything.
- State the legal subject directly. Reserve English process commentary for the editorial record.
- Use a relationship-specific layout before reaching for a card or a table. Tables remain valid where actual row-by-column comparison is the relationship.
- Preserve complete source access and check coverage separately from visual quality.
- Do not label a new design successful until the user has reviewed it.
